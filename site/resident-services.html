Looking for 311?

For Durham, North Carolina, use Durham One Call at 919-560-1200 or its online request form for the kinds of City services often called “311.” Phone hours are Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m., excluding holidays. Online requests are not monitored as an emergency dispatch service.

Water & sewer
