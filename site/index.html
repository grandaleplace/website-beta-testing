Welcome, neighbor

A South Durham
community.

Grandale Place is a neighborhood of 136 homes in South Durham, where the first home was built in 2000. A playground, green spaces, and shaded streets are part of everyday life here.

Our HOA helps care for common spaces and community standards. Find information and ways to get involved here, whether you’ve just moved in or have called Grandale Place home for years.
