Architectural review (ARC)How do I request approval for an exterior project?

Request written approval before starting an exterior improvement, alteration, grading or color change. Submit a complete plan showing the location, dimensions, elevations, colors and materials, plus any additional information the committee requests. Government permits are separate from HOA review. Article X.E describes a conditional 30-day review provision after a complete submission and written request; confirm receipt and status with management rather than assuming a partial application is approved.

Source: Article X.B–F. PDF page 29 PDF page 30

Mailbox guidelinesCan I replace or change my mailbox?

The recorded covenants allow a mailbox identical to the one originally provided by the developer without additional committee consent. A different mailbox requires committee consent. Ask management to confirm the original specification before buying a replacement; the document does not identify a particular current vendor or paint code.

Source: Article XI.W. PDF page 36

Parking and street issuesWhere can vehicles be parked?

On your lot, park on areas improved for parking, such as the garage, driveway or approved parking pad—not the lawn. The covenants restrict campers, mobile house trailers, commercial vehicles and tractor-trailers on lots and streets, and boats or boat trailers on streets. Boats kept on a lot must be screened with committee-approved screening that meets City requirements. Abandoned or inoperative vehicles may not be visible from other lots, streets or open space. This section does not state a blanket ban on ordinary passenger-car street parking. Contact management about covenant concerns and Resident Services for City street issues.

Source: Article XI.E. PDF page 32

Garbage bin placementWhen should bins go out and come back in?

Put collection items at the front of the lot after 5 p.m. on the day before your scheduled pickup, and remove the bins on collection day. Between pickups, store containers inside the home or in a fenced area, screened from view, clean and sanitary. The covenants also require containers to be substantially concealed from street and neighboring-property views through approved enclosure or landscaping. Check the collection calendar for holiday changes.

Source: Article XI.G and Q. PDF page 32 PDF page 34

PetsWhat do the recorded documents say about pets?

The recorded text permits common household pets, up to three per home with prior Board consent, and prohibits breeding or commercial use. It also contains species and breed restrictions, including a pit-bull restriction, and prohibits unreasonable pet-related nuisance. The Board may adopt additional rules on leashes, designated areas and cleanup. Ask management about current rules and any applicable accommodation before relying on these older provisions. Bag waste and use the neighborhood stations as a considerate everyday practice.

Source: Article XI.K. PDF page 33 PDF page 34

TreesWhat should I check before removing a tree?

Check your recorded plat and consult the City before removing or disturbing trees. A 25-foot Tree Save Easement affects some lots; in that area the covenants require preservation of trees exceeding six-inch caliper. The plat also sets street-tree requirements. Confirm whether an easement, drainage area or City protection applies to your specific tree before arranging removal.

Source: Article XI.Z. PDF page 36

LandscapingWhat upkeep and landscaping approvals are expected?

Maintain the landscaping on your lot and the adjoining strip up to the road pavement, including mowing, trimming, weed control and replacing dead or diseased plants. The recorded text calls for primarily grass landscaping, requires written approval before paving or covering landscaped areas with gravel or artificial surfaces, and prohibits artificial vegetation. Landscape improvements are subject to architectural review. Do not alter drainage or protected wetlands; check approvals and current watering restrictions before planning work.

Source: Article IX.B.2–3; Article X; Article XI.T. PDF page 28 PDF page 29 PDF page 35

FencesCan I install or replace a fence?

Obtain architectural approval and meet City requirements before installing a fence. The recorded text limits fences relative to front and side building setbacks and prohibits chain-link or other metal fencing, with an exception for 2-by-4-inch mesh used with split-rail fencing to contain animals. It includes exceptions for certain retaining walls and developer-installed fences. A proposal to expand permitted fence types is described in the voting notice; it has not been treated as an adopted change here.

Fence amendment voting closes October 31, 2026, at 5 p.m. Eastern. Read the voting notice.

Source: Article XI.V; Article X. PDF page 35 PDF page 29

Play structuresDo I need approval for a playset or other backyard structure?

The covenants do not give a separate size, color or setback rule specifically for residential playsets. Their broad architectural-review requirements apply to improvements and structures, so submit the proposed location, dimensions and materials for review before installation. Check drainage and easements and keep outdoor equipment neat. The separate provision about association playgrounds describes common-area use; it is not a blanket approval for private play structures.

Source: Article X.B–E; Article XI.D; Article II.D.5. PDF page 29 PDF page 30 PDF page 32 PDF page 10
